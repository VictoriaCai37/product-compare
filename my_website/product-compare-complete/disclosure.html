    Affiliate Disclosure

    Transparency about how we fund this site.

    How We're Funded

    GearLab is an independent review site. We purchase most products with our own funds for testing. To keep the site running without ads or paywalls, we participate in affiliate programs.

    Affiliate Programs We Use

    • Amazon Associates (US) — We earn from qualifying purchases on Amazon.com
    • Impact (Brand Partnerships) — Direct partnerships with brands like Anker, Dreame, Roborock, Ninja, Philips Hue, and others
    • AliExpress Portals — For budget-friendly international options

    What This Means for You

    • You pay the same price — commissions come from the retailer, not your pocket
    • We never accept free products in exchange for positive reviews
    • Our rankings are based on hands-on testing and data, not commission rates
    • Products scoring below our standards are excluded regardless of affiliate availability

    FTC Compliance

    In accordance with the Federal Trade Commission's guidelines on endorsements and testimonials, we disclose that we may earn a commission when you click links to retailers and make a purchase. This disclosure applies to all pages on this site.

    Editorial Independence

    Our testing methodology, scoring criteria, and pick selections are determined solely by our editorial team. Retailers and brands have zero influence on which products we test, how we score them, or which ones we recommend.

    Data & Price Accuracy

    Prices and availability are accurate as of the "Updated" date shown on each page. Prices can change without notice. We use automated APIs (Amazon PA-API, Impact) where possible, but always verify manually before publishing.
